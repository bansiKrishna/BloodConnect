Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a blood donor?

Simply click on "Register as Donor" and complete the registration form. You must be at least 17 years old and meet our health requirements.

How can my hospital partner with BloodConnect?

Hospitals and blood banks can register through our "Register as Facility" page. Our team will review your application within 48 hours.

Is there a mobile app available?

Yes! Our mobile app is available on both iOS and Android. Search for "BloodConnect" in your app store.

How do I request emergency blood?

Registered hospitals can use our emergency request feature in the dashboard. For public inquiries, call our 24/7 hotline.
