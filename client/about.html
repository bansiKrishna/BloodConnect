About BloodConnect

Connecting donors, hospitals, and blood banks to save lives through technology and compassion.

Our Story

BloodConnect was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: to ensure that no patient dies due to lack of blood. What started as a small initiative has grown into a nationwide network connecting thousands of donors with hospitals and blood banks.

Our platform leverages cutting-edge technology to streamline the blood donation process, from donor registration to blood distribution. We believe that every drop counts, and every donor is a hero.

Today, BloodConnect serves over 150 partner hospitals, has facilitated more than 50,000 blood donations, and has saved countless lives across the country.

Our Mission

To create a seamless, technology-driven blood donation ecosystem that ensures timely access to safe blood for every patient in need, while promoting a culture of regular blood donation.

Our Vision

A world where no life is lost due to blood shortage. We envision a future where every eligible person donates blood regularly, and every hospital has 24/7 access to adequate blood supplies.
